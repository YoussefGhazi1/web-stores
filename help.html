Help Center

Find answers to the most common questions about our products,orders,delivery,payment and website.

details> How can I place an order?

Choose the product you want, open its product page, and follow the instructions to complete your order.

How can I find a specific product?

You can browse the different categories on the Home page to find makeup, skincare, body care and other products.

Are the products available in different sizes?

Some products are available in different sizes. Check the product page for the available options.

How can I know the price of a product?

The product price is displayed directly on the product card and on the product details page.

What payment methods are available?

Available payment methods may vary. Please check the checkout page for the available payment options.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location and the delivery service. The expected delivery time will be provided when placing your order.

Can I cancel my order?

If you want to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible through the Contact page.

Can I return a product?

Return policies may depend on the product and its condition. Please contact our support team for more information.

How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our customer support team through the Contact page by filling out the contact form.

How can I get more information about a product?

Open the product card to visit its product page. There you can find more information about the product, including its description and price.
